CELL STRUCTURE — QUICK NOTES
============================

Definition:
Cell is the basic structural and functional unit of life.

--------------------------------------------------

Types of Cells:
- Prokaryotic (no nucleus, simple)
- Eukaryotic (true nucleus, complex)

--------------------------------------------------

Cell Organelles:
- Nucleus: controls activities
- Mitochondria: produces energy (ATP)
- Ribosomes: protein synthesis
- Endoplasmic Reticulum: transport system
- Golgi Apparatus: packaging and secretion

--------------------------------------------------

Cell Membrane:
- Semi-permeable
- Controls entry and exit of substances

--------------------------------------------------

Cytoplasm:
- Jelly-like substance where reactions occur

--------------------------------------------------

Importance:
- Maintains life processes
- Helps in growth and reproduction

--------------------------------------------------

Summary:
Cell = basic unit + contains organelles for functions
