RESPIRATION — QUICK NOTES
=========================

Definition:
Process by which organisms break down food to release energy.

--------------------------------------------------

Types:
- Aerobic (with oxygen)
- Anaerobic (without oxygen)

--------------------------------------------------

Equation (Aerobic):
C6H12O6 + O2 -> CO2 + H2O + Energy (ATP)

--------------------------------------------------

Steps:
1. Inhalation of oxygen
2. Breakdown of glucose
3. Release of energy
4. Exhalation of CO2

--------------------------------------------------

Importance:
- Provides energy for activities
- Maintains body functions

--------------------------------------------------

Summary:
Food + Oxygen -> Energy + CO2 + H2O
