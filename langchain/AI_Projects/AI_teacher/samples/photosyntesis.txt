PHOTOSYNTHESIS — QUICK NOTES
===========================

Definition:
Process by which green plants make food using sunlight, CO2, and H2O.

--------------------------------------------------

Equation:
6CO2 + 6H2O --(light + chlorophyll)--> C6H12O6 + 6O2

--------------------------------------------------

Requirements:
- Sunlight
- Chlorophyll
- Carbon dioxide (from air)
- Water (from soil)

--------------------------------------------------

Steps:
1. Roots absorb water
2. Leaves take in CO2 (through stomata)
3. Chlorophyll traps sunlight
4. Chemical reaction produces glucose
5. Oxygen is released

--------------------------------------------------

Products:
- Glucose (food)
- Oxygen (released)

--------------------------------------------------

Importance:
- Helps plants make food
- Releases oxygen for living beings
- Base of food chain

--------------------------------------------------

Key Terms:
- Stomata: tiny pores in leaves
- Chloroplast: site of photosynthesis
- Glucose: plant food

--------------------------------------------------

Summary:
Sunlight + CO2 + H2O -> Glucose + O2
